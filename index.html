The company

GameStop Corp. is a Grapevine, Texas–based specialty retailer of video games, gaming hardware, accessories, and collectibles, operating stores and e-commerce in the United States and several international markets. Its roots trace to Babbage's, a Dallas software retailer founded in 1984; the company went public on the NYSE in February 2002.

Since 2021, under a board and later CEO led by Ryan Cohen, GameStop has focused on cost cutting, closing underperforming stores, building a large cash position through at-the-market equity offerings, and — from 2025 — adding bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset. Brands include GameStop, EB Games, and Game Informer (the magazine shut down in 2024).

The story so far

  1. 1984

    Babbage's opens in Dallas

    The software retailer that eventually becomes GameStop is founded by James McCurley and Gary Kusin.

  2. Feb 2002

    IPO on the NYSE

    GameStop is spun out of Barnes & Noble's control and lists under the ticker GME.

  3. 2005

    Electronics Boutique acquired

    The merger makes GameStop the largest dedicated video-game retailer, adding the EB Games brand.

  4. Aug 2020

    Ryan Cohen takes a stake

    The Chewy co-founder's RC Ventures discloses a roughly 10% position and starts pushing for a digital pivot.

  5. Jan 2021

    The short squeeze

    Cohen and two allies join the board (Jan 11). Retail buying, much of it organized on Reddit's r/wallstreetbets, collides with heavy short interest. On Jan 28 GME hits an intraday high of $483 (pre-split), and several brokers, including Robinhood, restrict buying.

  6. Feb 2021

    Congress holds hearings

    The House Financial Services Committee questions Robinhood, Citadel, Melvin Capital, Reddit and Keith Gill (“Roaring Kitty”).

  7. Jun 2021

    Cohen becomes Chairman

    The board turns over and the company shifts toward e-commerce and cost discipline.

  8. Oct 2021

    SEC staff report

    The SEC publishes its staff report on equity and options market structure during the early-2021 meme-stock episode.

  9. Jul 2022

    4-for-1 stock split

    Done as a stock dividend; pre-split prices divide by four (so $483 ≈ $120.75 split-adjusted).

  10. Sep 2023

    Cohen named CEO

    Ryan Cohen adds president and CEO to his Chairman role.

  11. May–Jun 2024

    Roaring Kitty returns

    Keith Gill's return to social media sparks another surge; GameStop raises billions through at-the-market share sales.

  12. Mar 2025

    Bitcoin treasury

    The board approves bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset, financed in part by convertible note offerings.
