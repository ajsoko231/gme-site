LVL 1 The Lore

WORLD 1-1

From Babbage's to the Big Board

GameStop's roots trace back to Babbage's, a Dallas software retailer founded in 1984. Barnes & Noble bought the chain in 1999 and renamed it GameStop. The company went public on the NYSE as GME in February 2002, and Barnes & Noble spun off its remaining stake to shareholders in 2004. A year later GameStop bought rival Electronics Boutique and became the world's biggest video-game retailer.

WORLD 2-1

The 2021 Short Squeeze

  1. Aug 2020 Ryan Cohen's RC Ventures discloses a ~10% stake.
  2. Jan 11, 2021 Cohen and two allies join the board. Retail traders on Reddit's r/wallstreetbets pile in.
  3. Late Jan 2021 Heavily shorted shares rocket. Short sellers take huge losses and the stock hits an intraday high near $483 on Jan 28 (pre-split).
  4. Jan 28, 2021 Robinhood and other brokers limit buying, which sparks public outrage.
  5. Feb 18, 2021 Congress holds hearings on the frenzy.
WORLD 3-1

The Ryan Cohen Era

Cohen co-founded Chewy. He became GameStop's chairman in June 2021 and its CEO in September 2023. Under him the company cut costs and closed stores, built up a large cash pile, and did a 4-for-1 stock split in July 2022. Its devoted retail base has stayed one of the most famous in market history.
